Pasta

This spaghetti al pomodoro is the quintessential classic Italian dish. Ripe fresh tomatoes, garlic and extra virgin olive oil create a delicate tomato sauce that clings perfectly to pasta and is light, flavorful and pure summer comfort.

Ingredients

  • Tomatoes
  • Olive Oil
  • Pasta
  • Cheese
  • Basil

Steps

  1. While the sauce cooks, boil the spaghetti until just shy of al dente. Reserve about 1 cup of the pasta cooking water, then drain.
  2. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce, tossing well to coat. Add splashes of the cup of the pasta water as needed to help the delicate tomato sauce cling.
  3. Remove from the heat, stir in the fresh basil leaves, and add parmesan cheese, tossing until the pasta is glossy and well-coated.
  4. Serve right away with extra cheese, basil, and a drizzle of extra-virgin olive oil.
