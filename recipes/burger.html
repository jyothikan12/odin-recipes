Veg Burger

This Burger Recipe shows how to make delicious veggie burgers from scratch. It includes homemade mixed vegetable patties that are pan fried until crisp. The burgers are layered with a spiced mayonnaise dressing and fresh toppings like cucumber, tomato and onion. This easy veg burger recipe is perfect for a quick snack or meal.

Ingredients

  • Mixed Vegetables
  • Spices
  • Burger Buns
  • Spiced Mayonnaise Dressing

Steps

  1. Prepare the mixed vegetable patties by combining the vegetables with spices and forming them into patties.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and fry the patties until they are golden brown and crispy.
  3. Toast the burger buns if desired.
  4. Assemble the burgers by placing the patties on the bottom buns, adding the spiced mayonnaise dressing, and topping with fresh vegetables.
  5. Serve immediately and enjoy!
