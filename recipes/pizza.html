Pizza

This Mediterranean Pizza is perfect as a healhy weeknight dinner! Made with spinach, black olives, artichokes, red onion, and crumbled feta, this quick pizza recipe is full of flavor. Vegetarian, delicious, and so easy to make!

Ingredients

  • Pizza Dough
  • Olive Oil
  • Mozzarella Cheese
  • Feta
  • Tomatoes
  • Parsley
  • Spinach
  • Red Onion
  • Garlic
  • Olives
  • Artichokes

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Make the pizza dough and bake for about 8 minutes.
  3. Drizzle the olive oil over the pizza.
  4. Top with minced garlic, mozarella, spinach, tomatoes, olives, artichokes, red onion slices, and crumbled feta.
  5. Add fresh or dried parsley on top.
  6. Bake for about 15-20 minutes, or until the vegetables begin to soften.
  7. Let cool, slice, and enjoy!
